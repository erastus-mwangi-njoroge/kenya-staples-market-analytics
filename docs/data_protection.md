# Data protection and responsible use

- This repository uses only **aggregate, public** data. It holds no names, phone numbers, national IDs or household records.
- If extended with survey microdata (e.g. household or farmer-level files): keep raw files outside the repository, never commit them, apply the publisher's terms, remove direct identifiers before analysis, suppress small cells (for example fewer than 10 households) in published tables, and share outputs only at aggregated level.
- Dashboards show market- and region-level figures only.
- Every published chart carries its source, period and the main caveat from `docs/methodology.md`.
