# Template: one-page brief for non-technical readers

Use this structure when turning `outputs/brief.md` into a PDF for managers, programme teams or partners.

1. **Bottom line (2 sentences).** What changed and what we recommend.
2. **Evidence (1 chart + 3 numbers).** One chart that answers the question; avoid dashboards-in-a-page.
3. **Where to look (table, max 5 rows).** Markets or regions needing attention, and why.
4. **How sure are we? (3 bullets).** Data-quality caveats in plain language; link to the DQ report.
5. **Decisions needed / next steps.** Who does what by when.

Style rules: define each indicator once in words; use KES and calendar months; round to what a decision needs; never show a number without its source and period.
